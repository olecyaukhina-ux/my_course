🇬🇧 English A2: Present Simple

Daily routines, habits, and general facts

📚 Quick Grammar Reminder

  • I / You / We / They: work | don't work | Do you work?
  • He / She / It: works | doesn't work | Does he work?

Tip: Remember to add -s or -es for He, She, and It in affirmative sentences!
